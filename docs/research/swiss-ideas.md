# Build Before Q-Day · Swiss and European shortlist

**Pick Restore Drill, Encrypted Apps.** Hand the judge a backup they make themselves. Let them disconnect the backup creator, recover the exact bytes in a second browser, and try again with the storage operator’s key. A bank can buy encrypted backup storage today. What it wants to know on the morning of an outage is whether the people who remain can actually restore it.

Research cut at 22 November 2026. Deadline **2 December, 23:59 UTC**. These are research hypotheses, with no claimed customer interviews, partnerships or completed transactions. The ranking assumes one solo builder, five days to make one journey work, and the remaining time for independent replay, evidence and submission. The local brief records registration and funding on Diana. Key registration and deployment by this entrant remain open gates. ## Where the demand comes from

- **European PQ migration.** The NIS Cooperation Group’s 23 June 2025 roadmap recommends that Member States start transitioning by end-2026 and move high-risk uses by end-2030. This is a roadmap aimed at Member States. A Swiss family office has no general 2030 legal deadline under it. On 30 November 2024, France’s ANSSI, Germany’s BSI and partners had already called out the exposure of long-lived secrets to collection now and decryption later. The UK NCSC’s 2025 timetable sets discovery and planning by 2028, highest-priority work by 2031 and completion by 2035. An archive created today makes a better encryption demo than a countdown to a hypothetical quantum computer. Signatures address a different risk, forged authority to change state. [EU roadmap and scope](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/post-quantum-cryptography) · [Joint statement](https://cyber.gouv.fr/en/publications/jointly-led-international-publications/transitioning-to-post-quantum-cryptography/) · [UK NCSC milestones](https://www.ncsc.gov.uk/guidance/pqc-migration-timelines)
- **Private banking and custody.** FINMA identifies third-party outages and access to critical data as operational risks. Its 2025 annual report records increasing attacks on providers with direct consequences for supervised institutions. EU DORA, applicable from 17 January 2025, calls for periodic restore testing in Article 12. MiCA (2023), Article 75, calls for custody policies and procedures to return customer assets or access. A Swiss private bank, its EU subsidiary, a Zug crypto custodian and an unregulated single-family office have different legal perimeters. A restore test has operational value across all four. [FINMA](https://report.finma.ch/2025/en/finmas-core-tasks/measures-for-promoting-stability) · [DORA](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2022/2554/oj) · [ESMA on custody](https://www.esma.europa.eu/publications-and-data/interactive-single-rulebook/mica/article-75-providing-custody-and) · [Federal overview of Crypto Valley](https://www.aboutswitzerland.eda.admin.ch/en/financial-centre-banks-and-insurance-companies)
- **Personal records and pension tracing.** Switzerland’s revised FADP took effect on 1 September 2023, including data protection by design and default. GDPR Articles 5 and 32 pair data minimisation with risk-appropriate security. On 8 October 2026 the Confederation confirmed a data leak at a software supplier used by federal pension fund PUBLICA. That establishes third-party exposure. It does not establish which encryption would have prevented that incident. Separately, the occupational pension Guarantee Fund reported 204,324 matched balances in 2023 excluding forgotten balances. Its Central Office helps people locate pillar 2 assets. Pillar 3a is a separate tied private savings arrangement. A single combined pension search would promise access the builder lacks. [FDPIC](https://www.edoeb.admin.ch/en/18112024-the-new-data-protection-act-in-figures) · [Privacy by design guidance](https://www.edoeb.admin.ch/dam/en/sd-web/eVhrh8wY3QcR/TOM_EN.pdf) · [GDPR](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj/eng/) · [PUBLICA incident](https://www.bakom.admin.ch/en/newnsb/FjG4XOIms04s) · [2023 Guarantee Fund report](https://sfbvg.ch/fileadmin/user_upload/sfbvg/Geschaeftsberichte/SF-GB-2023-EN.pdf) · [Pillar 3a](https://www.ch.ch/fr/travail/prevoyance-vieillesse/prevoyance-privee-3eme-pilier)
- **Identity and cantonal trust.** Swiss voters approved the federal e-ID Act on 28 September 2025 with 50.39% yes. The Confederation issues the credential and operates trust infrastructure for swiyu. Under the separate EU framework of 11 April 2024, wallet relying parties declare intended use and the data requested. Romania’s interior ministry presented RO Wallet progress on 6 October 2026. A small project could let a citizen see a request expand across languages, without claiming to issue an official credential. Swiss Post e-voting trials likewise have a specifically authorised, independently examined system; the Chancellery published a cantonal examination on 23 June 2025. A Diana vote receipt would establish neither ballot secrecy nor election verifiability. Leave voting off the shortlist. [Swiss vote](https://www.eid.admin.ch/en/e-id-gesetz-an-der-urne-angenommen-e) · [eIDAS amendment](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1183/oj) · [Romanian ministry](https://www.mai.gov.ro/comunicat-de-presa-1247/) · [Swiss e-voting examinations](https://www.bk.admin.ch/en/examination-of-systems)

## Ten ideas checked against the 129-entry gallery

Closest means the nearest *described* mechanic in the 22 November snapshot. Several listings disclose little. Sector examples below are proposed workflows. The linked sources document the underlying problem.

### 1. Restore Drill · Encrypted Apps

When a private bank’s continuity officer runs a restore drill in a clean browser, they get the original file back with its checksum verified while the backup host holds only ciphertext, and QoreChain gets a recoverability test using a Diana-registered recovery key.

The producer seals a judge-supplied file locally to the independent recovery operator’s ML-DSA-bound ML-KEM key. Export the encrypted file and a manifest with the recipient key fingerprint and ciphertext digest. At restore time, verify the pinned key fingerprint against its confirmed Diana registration before marking the drill passed. If that proof is unavailable, report byte recovery and leave recipient verification pending. Disable registry verification at creation and the app must refuse to create the bank-addressed backup. Closest is **coffer**, which delivers environment secrets to wallets. The distinctive artifact here is a repeatable cold restore after the source browser goes away, including an independently kept expected checksum and a wrong-key run. [DORA Art. 12](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2022/2554/oj) · [coffer](https://build.qorechain.io/p/coffer)

### 2. Frontier Days · Encrypted Apps

When a French resident working in Geneva closes their work-year calendar, they get an employer-ready day count while their daily whereabouts stay on their device, and QoreChain gets a tax-workflow handoff sealed to the employer’s registered key.

The Franco-Swiss amendment applies from 1 January 2026. The 40% rule and treatment of temporary missions make “I worked from home sometimes” an inadequate record. Support one ordinary full-year case with days in Switzerland or telework at home in France. Flag missions, partial days and ambiguous entries for payroll review. Locally derive the count, show the exact fields to be sent, and QoreSeal only those fields to the employer. Without verified key lookup, withhold the export. Closest is **Cista**. Here the worker sends an annual aggregate upstream and the employer can never decrypt a daily route from the packet. The app prepares inputs to the employer’s process. [Geneva guidance](https://www.ge.ch/imposition-du-teletravail-personnes-frontalieres/accords-applicables-au-teletravail) · [Federal employer form notes](https://www.estv.admin.ch/dam/fr/sd-web/sYfqlykWLoU6/Avenant-CH-FR-Explications-Attestation-employeur-fr.pdf) · [Cista](https://build.qorechain.io/p/cista)

### 3. Watch Recall · Post-Quantum dApp

When a Geneva watch owner flags a missing watch passport, they get a transfer freeze that a second-hand dealer can check before purchase, and QoreChain gets a PQ-authorised stop on the resale of a long-lived digital asset.

An enrolled demo passport has an opaque random ID. A contract verifies the owner’s ML-DSA instruction bound to chain, contract, ID, action, nonce and expiry. A freeze invalidates outstanding transfer offers and blocks new transfers until a separately authorised recovery. Without the registered key check, someone with ordinary EVM signing access could clear the flag. Closest is **QNS**, which guards transfers and changes to a name. The wedge is an outstanding dealer offer that becomes unexecutable when the watch is reported missing. Richemont launched Enquirus on 30 March 2023; its retail service already supports stolen-watch lookups. Demonstrate the *transfer stop* on the demo passport. Physical authenticity, legal title and access to the Enquirus registry remain outside the proof. [Richemont launch](https://www.richemont.com/news-media/press-releases-news/richemont-launches-enquirus/) · [Retailer workflow](https://enquirus.com/en/partners/pre-owned-market-retailers) · [QNS](https://build.qorechain.io/p/qns-qore-name-service)

### 4. Pension Trace Reply · Encrypted Apps

When a former employee opens a pension-tracing reply, they get the name of the institution to contact while the forwarding service sees only an opaque case token, and QoreChain gets a long-lived personal record delivered through recipient key binding.

A claimant supplies a per-case address through a separately authenticated intake. A demo desk seals a structured institution contact card to that address. Suppress registry validation and the desk cannot safely dispatch. Closest is **Agama** (sensitive financial files). Here the intermediary routes an answer without learning the matched institution, and the packet contains neither a balance nor a loan dossier. Show that delivery property with two local roles. Only an authorised institution could make a real match. [Central Office tracing procedure](https://sfbvg.ch/fileadmin/user_upload/sfbvg/SF.5_ZS_Suche/SF-F5-MB-EN.pdf) · [October 2026 supplier leak](https://www.bakom.admin.ch/en/newnsb/FjG4XOIms04s) · [Agama](https://build.qorechain.io/p/agama)

### 5. Basel Publication Pair · Encrypted Apps

When a clinical-trial disclosure lead releases a redacted protocol, they get a public copy paired with a recoverable confidential original for an authorised reviewer, and QoreChain gets a verifiable recipient binding for the long-lived original.

EMA revised CTIS transparency rules on 18 June 2024. Earlier publication and redaction put the public/private version distinction directly in the operator’s hands. Use two explicitly supplied files and a local public preview. Seal the original to the registered reviewer with the public digest inside the envelope. Swap in an unrelated public copy and verification fails. Closest is **SealedMed Referral**. The new proof is detecting a mismatched public/private publication pair. Use a public document and a judge-edited demo variant. Actual redaction decisions and CTIS submission stay with the sponsor. EU trial master files generally have a 25-year minimum retention under Article 58. That does not give every Swiss medical record the same retention period. [EMA transparency changes](https://www.ema.europa.eu/system/files/documents/presentation/07revised-ctis-transparency-rules-new-version-public-portal_en.pdf) · [Clinical Trials Regulation](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2014/536/oj) · [Basel industry context, 2023](https://www.aboutswitzerland.eda.admin.ch/en/chemical-and-pharmaceutical-industry) · [SealedMed](https://build.qorechain.io/p/sealedmed-referral)

### 6. One Parcel · Post-Quantum dApp

When a Vaud property owner authorises a notary to request one restricted extract, they get a proxy redeemable once for that parcel and extract type, and QoreChain gets a scoped-window transaction exercising a PQ-signed mandate.

Vaud accepts an owner’s procuration for certified extracts. Build a mock document desk which releases a demonstration extract only after the named recipient redeems the matching opaque commitment. Bind recipient, parcel commitment, extract type, expiry and nonce into the verified mandate. A second parcel or second redemption fails on chain. Closest is **QoreStamp**. Its stamp proves an attestation; this application consumes a tightly specified power to obtain a particular document. The cantonal office retains responsibility for identity and lawful access. [Vaud service, consulted 22 Nov 2026](https://www.vd.ch/prestation/commander-un-extrait-certifie-conforme-du-registre-foncier) · [QoreStamp](https://build.qorechain.io/p/qorestamp-2)

### 7. Voyage Cost Annex · Encrypted Apps

When a Geneva commodities clerk answers a voyage-cost query, they get one voyage’s itemised ancillary costs readable by the designated reviewer, and QoreChain gets selective PQ delivery of commercially sensitive trading records.

SECO’s 2025 interpretation points operators to oil-price-cap guidance on attestations, recordkeeping and itemised ancillary costs. Generate separate envelopes per voyage and bind the voyage reference and attestation version inside. A recipient for voyage A cannot open B. Closest is **TravelSeal**, which exchanges travel-rule information between exchanges. The wedge is resolving a contested voyage’s freight and insurance totals without disclosing the rest of a charter book. Calculating a lawful cap or certifying sanctions compliance would require much more evidence. [SECO guidance](https://www.seco.admin.ch/dam/seco/de/dokumente/Aussenwirtschaft/Wirtschaftsbeziehungen/Exportkontrollen/Sanktionen/auslegungshilfe_sanktionsmassnahmen_2025-10-30.pdf.download.pdf/Auslegungshilfe%20f%C3%BCr%20Sanktionsmassnahmen.pdf) · [Geneva/Zug trading figures, 18 Nov 2025](https://www.eda.admin.ch/en/newnsb/Pv9kCk1ioIAR6duJhxciN) · [TravelSeal](https://build.qorechain.io/p/travelseal)

### 8. Return Queue · AI Agents

When a Zug custody operator starts a client-return rehearsal, they get an agent that returns test assets only to the clients’ pre-authorised destinations, and QoreChain gets authenticated agent instructions, bounded execution and independently checkable balance changes.

For a custodian serving an EU entity, MiCA provides a specific operational test. A signed client instruction fixes amount, destination, nonce and expiry. The agent picks the next valid claim, reserves it through an ordinary, target/time/count/native-value scoped window, then executes the return through a selector-limited linked key and smart-account spending cap. A contract binds both steps to the same claim and balances. The two paths must each do real work. Closest is **Steward**. Clients are reclaiming their own segregated balances, so two competing returns must never consume the same entitlement. Highest integration cost of the ten. [MiCA Art. 75](https://www.esma.europa.eu/publications-and-data/interactive-single-rulebook/mica/article-75-providing-custody-and) · [Agent rules](https://build.qorechain.io/faq#agents) · [Steward](https://build.qorechain.io/p/steward)

### 9. Same Request · Post-Quantum dApp

When a Romanian service operator activates a bilingual identity request, they get one approved attribute list that a verifier applies in both languages, and QoreChain gets an ML-DSA-authorised change to a relying party’s disclosure policy.

A demo wallet derives the request from canonical attribute IDs and approved translation digests. Only a verified policy update through a scoped window can add a field. Replace “over 18” with “date of birth” or change a translation file and the demo wallet refuses that request. Closest is **T-REX ID**, which centres on investor identity and eligibility. This protects the scope of the *requester’s* policy. It neither establishes the truth of the attributes nor requires a connection to swiyu or RO Wallet. [eIDAS 2, Art. 5b, 2024](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1183/oj) · [Romanian wallet update, Oct 2026](https://www.mai.gov.ro/comunicat-de-presa-1247/) · [T-REX ID](https://build.qorechain.io/p/t-rex-id-quantum-safe-erc-3643-institutional-ide)

### 10. Migration Rehearsal · Quantum Pass

When a UK infrastructure supplier retires a legacy signer in a Diana rehearsal, they get a verifier that accepts the replacement for the next release and refuses the old key, and QoreChain gets a hybrid-signed retirement that a downstream operator can reproduce.

Start with two real demo signer keys. Use a scaffolded QoreX-connected app, deployed epoch registry and a verified hybrid governance action binding the new key and cutover epoch. The downstream verifier reads the confirmed policy and checks a fresh challenge. If it cannot verify the cutover it must refuse the new release. Closest is **Q-Day Readiness Audit**. The payoff is a second operator’s working acceptance test across cutover. The UK’s 2028 discovery target and the EU high-risk 2030 milestone make this relevant to suppliers on both sides of the Channel. ## Finalists and the evidence that would earn the pitch

Scores are prospective. **Sponsor** asks what breaks without the sponsor primitive. **Both halves** asks whether one action also exercises that primitive. **Gate** covers track eligibility and live integration. **Real** asks whether a judge can reproduce the claimed outcome on Diana with actual keys, transactions and fresh inputs. **Buildable** asks whether one solo journey can work in five days. A 2 means credible within scope, 1 means a material dependency remains, 0 means the concept fails that test. None has earned a 2 for gate through a live spike yet.

### Restore Drill

**Winners post, if demonstrated.** “A judge backed up a file, closed the source browser and recovered identical bytes on a second device. QoreSeal protected the archive for the recovery key registered on Diana, so the storage operator’s copy and its own key were insufficient to read it.”

**One screenshot.** A single drill result showing the input and recovered SHA-256 side by side, byte count, wrong-key decryption failure and clickable recipient-registration transaction. Show a fresh filename chosen by the judge. For a two-device claim, perform the test on two devices; two isolated profiles suffice for the narrower clean-browser claim.

**Riskiest call.** Resolve the recovery address on Diana, verify the ML-DSA binding and use that exact public key in browser QoreSeal. Import the independently provisioned recovery secret into an isolated profile and decrypt after the creator has closed. Do this first with 32 random bytes. Never bundle the recovery secret with the backup. Pin the tested key version for existing archives. PQ encryption protects captured ciphertext under its key assumptions; anyone who obtains the recovery secret can decrypt. The registry transaction establishes the recipient binding. The checksum comparison establishes recovery.

**Swiss judge / five days.** A private bank can outsource storage and still own the consequence of a failed restore. The proof is legible even to a non-cryptographer. Day 1 establishes binding and recovery, day 2 exports a portable encrypted file, day 3 runs the cold restore, day 4 adds the independent checksum and negative case, day 5 repeats with a new judge-supplied file. No bank integration. [FINMA findings](https://report.finma.ch/2025/en/finmas-core-tasks/measures-for-promoting-stability) · [QoreSeal](https://github.com/qorechain/qoreseal) · [Official workshop](https://docs.qorechain.io/build-before-qday/workshop-3/)

### Frontier Days

**Winners post, if demonstrated.** “A cross-border worker turned a calendar into a year-end summary that their employer could open. The employee could inspect every field before QoreSeal encrypted the summary for the employer’s Diana-registered key, and the daily calendar stayed local.”

**One screenshot.** Split employee/employer view of the same run showing the local days by category, exhaustive outbound field preview, decrypted totals, recipient fingerprint and registration receipt. Use judge-entered dates and demo identities. Show a mission day triggering review. Never label an illustrative count a filed tax attestation.

**Riskiest call.** Resolve and authenticate the employer’s registered encryption key in the employee browser. Verify that the decrypted payload in a separate employer profile contains only the previewed fields and that changing the recipient prevents decryption. The party controlling each account is identified out of band. Keep registration transactions public, daily facts and aggregate data off chain.

**Swiss judge / five days.** A judge in Rolle can recognise a French-resident colleague’s year-end question. The January 2026 rule makes the workflow timely. Day 1 proves the same QoreSeal gate, days 2–3 implement one working pattern and review exceptions, days 4–5 verify the exact export and repeat across profiles. This places second because the arithmetic can become the whole demo unless the judge sees what the employer actually receives. [Geneva’s applicable agreements](https://www.ge.ch/imposition-du-teletravail-personnes-frontalieres/accords-applicables-au-teletravail)

### Watch Recall

**Winners post, if demonstrated.** “A demo watch owner reported a passport missing on Diana while a dealer still held an outstanding transfer offer. The dealer’s transfer failed and the passport stayed frozen until the owner authorised recovery with their registered post-quantum key.”

**One screenshot.** One passport timeline showing offer issued → PQ freeze confirmed → attempted acceptance refused, with owner and status read back from the contract. Link freeze receipt, open-window receipt and refusal evidence. Mark simulations as simulations. After recovery, test that the pre-freeze offer still fails, so recovery cannot silently revive it.

**Riskiest call.** Use the sponsor’s exact precompile ABI and digest format for a replay-resistant freeze/recovery instruction. With a valid scoped EVM window open, try a classical-only recovery, a stale offer and a legitimate fresh instruction. The contract must check signer ownership and invalidate offers by incrementing the passport epoch. The hybrid window-opening receipt and the contract-level PQ check prove distinct properties.

**Swiss judge / five days.** The Geneva watch trade already asks whether a piece has been reported stolen. Watching a live dealer offer fail gives that question a consequence. Day 1 spikes the precompile, day 2 implements one owner, one dealer and one passport, day 3 runs adversarial Foundry tests and QCAI stage 1, day 4 fixes findings, day 5 retests on Diana and runs stage 2. Retain both dated PDFs and the finding-by-finding response in docs/AUDIT.md. A digital stop has force only inside participating systems. [Richemont on Geneva and Paris use](https://www.richemont.com/about-us/partnerships/) · [Audit FAQ](https://build.qorechain.io/faq#audit)

**Selection rule.** Start with the 32-byte Restore Drill test. A real key-registration hash plus independent, successful browser recovery buys more confidence than a polished archive dashboard. Frontier Days shares that dependency and is an alternative audience story if the primitive works. If browser key registration or decapsulation is blocked, Watch Recall gives the builder an independent technical route. For either encrypted finalist the track evidence includes ML-KEM-1024, registry lookup, ML-DSA binding, and client-side encryption and decryption. Use a newly registered test recovery/employer key for a fresh gallery-verifiable transaction. Avoid putting identifying case labels, calendars, customer documents or plaintext digests of predictable personal records on Diana.
