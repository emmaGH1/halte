# main post

picture this.

you're a watch dealer. the seller puts a watch and its papers on your counter. the stolen-items register comes back clean. do you pay?

that's the problem. registers vary by country and only show what's been reported and added. papers can be faked.

so i built halte for @QoreChain's build before q-day hackathon.

each watch gets a passport tied to its owner's post-quantum key. one https call or contract read checks it. the owner signs a theft report from their phone. from the next block, every check says "do not buy." deals made before the report stay dead after recovery.

• dealers check before paying\
• marketplaces check before listing\
• insurers can check the same theft status

why qorechain? it checks the ml-dsa-87 signature onchain. after q-day, a thief with a cracked wallet key still can't report, unfreeze or sell.

honest limit: diana testnet only. halte holds the demo keys so you can try it without a wallet. hold your own key at /own. the passport shows what the chain says. you still have to match it to the watch on the counter.

stolen watches, unsellable. what would you check before paying? 👇
