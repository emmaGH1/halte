# AI usage

Halte was built by one person working with AI coding agents.

- **Claude Code (Anthropic)** wrote most of the code (contract, tests, scripts, web app), ran the deploys and the on-chain proof runs, and drafted the docs. The human chose the idea, approved the plan and the journey checks, made the product calls and reviewed the results on a phone.
- **Codex (OpenAI)** acted as an independent reviewer: a full teardown of the build, a disagreement round and an agreement (`docs/review/`). Its findings changed the contract (epoch bump on transfer, proof-of-possession checks, the dealer key) and the demo (a positive control before every "refused").
- **QCAI** (QoreChain's AI auditor) audited the contract in two stages; responses are in `docs/AUDIT.md`.

Progress was measured by checks the human approved (`checks/journey.mjs`, run against the live site by `scripts/status.mjs`), not by the agents' own reports. No transactions, results or metrics shown in the app or the docs were generated by an AI: they are read from QoreChain Diana.
