# Halte homepage variations

## 1st · Verification Desk

[Open the page](2-verification-desk/index.html) · [Phone hero](2-verification-desk/shots/phone-hero.png) · [Full phone page](2-verification-desk/shots/phone-full.png) · [Desktop hero](2-verification-desk/shots/desk-hero.png)

A dealer sees the watch passport and its verdict together before paying. The engraved credential, illustrated steel bracelet and spare serif typography feel appropriate at a watch counter. This is my first choice because the visitor can identify both the object and the decision before reading the explanation. The first verdict is positive, so the refusal replay carries the theft story.

## 2nd · Sapphire Vault

[Open the page](1-sapphire-vault/index.html) · [Phone hero](1-sapphire-vault/shots/phone-hero.png) · [Full phone page](1-sapphire-vault/shots/phone-full.png) · [Desktop hero](1-sapphire-vault/shots/desk-hero.png)

A laminated sapphire passport makes the owner’s digital credential feel like something they hold. The engraved rosette, polished edges and separated layers give the hero depth. This direction gives Halte a distinctive object for future product imagery. A first-time visitor needs the supporting sentence to understand the stolen-watch check.

A safety-yellow inspection plate puts the contract’s refusal front and center. It has the clearest theft headline, though the padlock alone could belong to several security categories.
