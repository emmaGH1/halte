Common questions.

How do I check a watch?

Send GET /api/check/{id}?offer=N with the passport ID and offer number. For an on-chain passport status read, call statusOf(id).

What happens when it’s found?

The owner signs a recovery. The passport becomes active again. An offer from before the theft report stays void, even if its timer is still running.

Who can use the verdict?

A dealer can check at the counter, a marketplace can check before checkout, and an insurer can read the same passport when reviewing a claim.

Where can I inspect the evidence?

The contract runs on the Diana testnet. Open the theft report transaction to inspect the signed call.
