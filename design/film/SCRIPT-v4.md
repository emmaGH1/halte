# Halte | Demo film

### 1. The scratch

**VO** Your dad used to say you could polish that scratch out of his watch.
When he gave it to you, you left it right where it was.

**Screen** A scratch catches the light at four o’clock on the dot-matrix dial.

### 2. The listing

**VO** Then somebody steals it. Three days later, there’s that scratch in a listing photo. A buyer sends a message. “I can pick it up tonight.”

**Screen** The dial becomes the listing photo as the buyer’s chat slides in.

### 3. Before money changes hands

**VO** Before they pay, there’s one thing they can check. Halte gives your watch a passport on QoreChain. You or the watchmaker can issue it, and it’s tied to your own post-quantum key.

**Screen** Open the passport card beside the listing.

### 4. Back on your phone

**VO** Go back to the moment you noticed it was missing. From your phone, you tap the button and sign once. QoreChain verifies your signature on chain. By the next block, the passport is frozen.

**Screen** Tap Report missing, then show the explorer receipt with the call labeled “pqcVerify · ML-DSA-87.”

### 5. At pickup

**VO** Now watch what the buyer sees. Refused. They keep their money. Even if the thief takes your watch to a dealer, one HTTPS call or one contract read gets the same answer straight from the chain.

**Screen** Hold “Refused · PassportFrozen” for a beat before the VO says “Refused,” then cut to the API call.

### 6. Back home

**VO** When you get your watch back, you can unfreeze it. An offer made before the theft stays dead. Even a deal somebody lined up while it was missing can’t go through.

**Screen** Show recovery, then hold “Sellable” beside the old offer’s “StaleOffer.”

### 7. The cracked key

**VO** Q-Day is when quantum computers can crack the keys today’s wallets use. Watch a thief try that key to authorize a sale. QoreChain refuses the signature. It checks for your post-quantum key on a sale, just as it does when you report your watch missing or lift the freeze.

**Screen** Hold the thief’s “BadPqcSignature” beside the passport’s ML-DSA-87 key label.

### 8. The receipt

**VO** Open the explorer. Every step is a real transaction on QoreChain’s Diana testnet that you can check yourself. Halte. Stolen watches, unsellable.

**Screen** Match the explorer receipt back to the scratched dial, then hold the Halte end card.

**Main VO** · 256 words · about 1:45 at 150 words per minute, including brief holds.

### Alternate opening beats

Each replaces beat 1 and cuts directly to beat 2.

**A | The first paycheck**

**VO** You set a little aside from every paycheck for that watch. The first scratch hurt enough that you still remember the doorframe that put it there.

**Screen** Paydays flip past until the board resolves into the dial with its scratch at four o’clock.

**B | Graduation**

**VO** In your graduation photo, your wrist is turned just enough to show the watch your mom gave you. That was before you put the scratch in it.

**Screen** A graduation date flips onto the board behind the dial, then the scratch catches the light.
