# Halte film direction

## What loses the shot

- The charcoal stage, window and passport share nearly the same value. At film scale the window edge disappears.
- Vermilion marks both the theft-report action and the refusal. The viewer has to read both to work out which one happened.
- Spaced uppercase mono gives navigation, field labels and technical evidence the same voice.
- The full-width history log makes the viewer hunt through previous events for the current dealer answer.
- The storyboard shrinks the whole app to fit the shot. The offer number and refusal reason become illegible before the viewer can connect them.

## Blue instrument dial

Use a flat enamel-blue stage, like the face of a sports watch seen straight on. Inside the passport, crop a single dial close enough to see the minute track, turned steel bezel and luminous inserts. Keep that crop, the serial and the offer in exactly the same positions on all three attempts.

| Token | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Enamel | #2859DB | Flat, edge-to-edge stage and chapter accent |
| Ink | #162D49 | Hands, type and buy button |
| Porcelain | #FFFFFF | Cards and pill |
| Case silver | #E8EEF3 | Window body |
| Lume | #C7FF86 | Hand inserts, cursor, caption emphasis and sellable signal |
| Ruby | #AE2151 | Frozen status and refusal signal |

Barlow Condensed Semibold (Google Fonts, BarlowCondensed-SemiBold.ttf) carries the 112 px verdict and 56 px serial like numerals printed on a dial. Switzer Medium (Fontshare, Switzer-500.woff2) carries the interface and caption. Both files ship beside the frame.

The window has a 30 px radius and a 0 34px 85px -24px #162D4980 shadow. Cards have a 22 px radius and a 0 10px 24px -12px #162D493B shadow. The 16 px button radius keeps its silhouette distinct from the pill.

For a sale, pair a dark check in a lume disc with “Sellable”. For a refusal, pair a porcelain bar in a ruby octagon with “Refused” and set the contract reason immediately beneath it. On recovery, switch the reason to StaleOffer and retain the stop shape.

Center the white chapter pill above the window. A silver inset holds 02, and only “freeze” takes enamel blue. Set the caption directly on the stage with one lume word. Park the glossy pearl 31 px inside the button’s right edge, clear of the label. At contact its round silhouette squashes to 51 × 42 px, with a white reflection at ten o’clock and a short shadow beneath.
