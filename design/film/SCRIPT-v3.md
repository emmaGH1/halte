# Halte film · script v3 (the Activation Lock framing)

About 230 words, roughly 1:45 at 150 wpm. The word "never" is not used.

1. Steal an iPhone today, and you end up holding a locked phone. It belongs to its owner's account, so it's worth almost nothing to anyone else.
2. Steal a twenty-thousand-dollar watch, and you end up holding twenty thousand dollars.
3. A stolen watch can be back on sale within days, and the buyer has no way to tell.
4. Phones fixed this with a lock that follows the owner. Watches have nothing like it.
5. Halte gives every watch that lock. The watchmaker issues a passport on QoreChain, tied to the owner's key.
6. That key is built for after Q-Day, when quantum computers break the cryptography most accounts rely on today. QoreChain checks it on chain with ML-DSA, a post-quantum signature.
7. Before anyone pays for the watch, they check its passport. Here, a dealer checks. Clear to buy.
8. Now the watch is stolen. The owner reports it from their phone with one signature, and the passport locks in the next block.
9. Same dealer, same watch. Refused.
10. When the watch comes home, the owner unlocks it. Any deal made while it was missing stays cancelled.
11. And the thief? Even with a cracked wallet key, the lock holds. Only the owner's ML-DSA key can open it.
12. For a watchmaker, it's a passport at the point of sale. For a dealer or marketplace, it's one check before paying.
13. Steal an iPhone, and you get a locked phone. Now steal a watch, and you get a locked watch.
14. Halte. Stolen watches, unsellable.
