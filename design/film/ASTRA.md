## Frame and performance

- Float a straight-on Mac-style window on matte graphite (#161619). One bone hairline at low opacity separates it from the #07070A app. Keep the surround empty except for the occasional evidence note. Preserve the real page proportions. Crop private control URLs from browser chrome.
- The cursor is a tiny bone dot-matrix watch hand with one brighter dot at its tip. On contact the shaft shortens by one dot; a single hollow dial index appears around the tip for four frames. Release restores its length. Align the tip to the captured click. It carries no status colour. Reserve #FF4526 for a stop and #5BD69A for a confirmed go.
- Founder’s ElevenLabs clone, close and dry, as if explaining a receipt across a counter. A single quiet tick sets the opening pace. Drop it when the hand stops. Keep the refusal holds quiet.
- Caption in short phrase groups in a dedicated lower matte, clear of the tour card. Use the app’s sans, #ECEAE4. Bring each word from muted to full bone on its spoken onset; give the cue word below a heavier weight. No bounce. Freeze the camera while anyone has to read.

## Edit risks and founder call

- **False continuity.** The opening lookup is the recorded example. The tour can use a different passport. Preserve that cut and use one tour passport throughout the three dealer checks. Keep its actual block numbers. Label compressed waits at the first edit.
- **A refusal that looks staged.** Use the app’s own verdict and error text. Keep the pointer clear of it. The explorer proves inclusion of the genuine transaction; the precompile name and byte sizes are editorial notes.
- **A luxury-watch cliché.** Skip stock wrists and metallic reflections. Test the three dealer shots at phone size with captions off. The button, status and verdict must carry the scene.
- **Founder call at rough cut.** Confirm the cloned voice’s pronunciation of Halte and QoreChain against your own read. Keep “ML-DSA-87” on screen while speaking the letters and “eighty-seven.” Check word timings against the approved take.
