# Four directions for Halte

Open any concept’s `index.html` locally. **See how it works** starts four short owner lessons and a seven-step tour. **Passport** jumps to an active offer. All actions replay a recorded QoreChain testnet journey locally. The watch designs and names are illustrative. The addresses, transaction links, QR destination and dealer check results belong to the recorded offer #2.

## 1 · [Calibre Folio](1-calibre-folio/index.html)

A sage-green sector dial, measured drawing and double-ruled certificate borrow the precision of a watchmaker’s reference plate.  
It suits collectors who inspect a movement before they read a specification, and insurers who want a readable chain of custody.  
The risk is quietness. The dealer’s refusal carries more urgency than the first impression.

## 2 · [Stop Order](2-stop-order/index.html)

A numbered dealer slip physically crosses the watch. The oversized VOID stamp previews what happens to the offer.  
Orange, sulphur-yellow paper and heavy rules give a busy counter clerk and a time-pressed judge a shared visual language.  
The risk is that VOID could initially read as a verdict on the watch itself. The dealer-copy label and certificate status distinguish the two.

## 3 · [Night Reserve](3-night-reserve/index.html)

An exploded sapphire dial hangs in a dark inspection chamber. A slow sweep of light crosses its four layers.  
Private dealers and collectors get the intimacy of examining a single piece under a loupe, with muted metalwork around the proof.  
The risk is distance. The illustration rewards a second look, while the product’s practical benefit depends on reading the headline.

## 4 · [Clasp Club](4-clasp-club/index.html)

A plum bracelet, pink dial and scalloped inspection charm turn the passport into a personal collection object.  
It welcomes a first-time watch owner. Rounded forms carry into the lessons and certificate, while the refused state gives a direct instruction.  
The risk is institutional fit. An insurer may find the charm language too informal for a loss report.

## Recommendation

**Stop Order** for the hackathon. A judge sees the object, the dealer’s copy and the consequence in one glance. Returning to the same numbered offer after recovery gives the recorded **59 min 44 s** a clear purpose. Calibre Folio is the strongest choice for an insurer-led launch.
